## Developer News

A new security advisory, [CVE-2026-19444 (kubectl cp path traversal on Windows allows arbitrary file writes)](https://groups.google.com/g/kubernetes-announce/c/kPN22R7cjZo), was published on September 28; a malicious tar archive can cause `kubectl cp` on Windows to write files outside the intended destination.

Shu Muto ([@shu-mutou](https://github.com/shu-mutou)) has [moved to SIG UI emeritus](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9189), following the earlier SIG UI leadership transitions covered in prior editions.

WG Node Identity has [added a dedicated Slack channel](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9178) for its working group discussions.

SIG ContribEx has [added vetting criteria for the #announcements Slack channel](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9184) to clarify what messages are appropriate for posting there.


### Election Update
* The [2026 Kubernetes Steering Committee election results](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/results.md) were announced at the public Steering meeting on October 7: [Michael McCune](https://github.com/elmiko), [Janet Kuo](https://github.com/janetkuo), and [Priyanka Saggu](https://github.com/priyankasaggu11929) were elected to the three open seats for two-year terms. 308 of 1547 eligible voters cast ballots, for a turnout of 19.91%.
