## Developer News

* SIG ContribEx has [published its 2025 annual report](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/8859), summarizing the year's community-infrastructure work, Slack and GitHub administration, mentoring programs, and contributor-experience investments.

* WG Device Management has [published its 2025 annual report](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/8834) covering the working group's progress on device plugin architecture and DRA-related work.

* SIG API Machinery has [added `apiextensions-api` to its server-crd subproject](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9234), bringing CRD API types into scope for the subproject's maintainers.

* The [Maintainer Summit at KubeCon NA 2026 is nearly full](https://groups.google.com/a/kubernetes.io/g/dev/c/5Q8l8ODq2RE); maintainers who plan to attend in Salt Lake City should register before capacity is reached.
