## Release Schedule

**Next Deadline: [Feature Blog Freeze](https://github.com/kubernetes/sig-release/blob/master/releases/release_phases.md#feature-blog-freeze), [November 4 (AoE)](https://github.com/kubernetes/sig-release/tree/master/releases/release-1.38#timeline)**

Contributors planning v1.38 feature blogs should open placeholder PRs by November 4. [Code and test freeze begin November 16 (AoE)](https://github.com/kubernetes/sig-release/tree/master/releases/release-1.38#timeline); prepare implementation and test PRs for review ahead of the freeze.

Kubernetes [v1.38.0-alpha.3](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-1.38.md#v1380-alpha3) is available.

Patch releases [1.37.2](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-1.37.md#v1372), [1.36.6](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-1.36.md#v1366), [1.35.10](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-1.35.md#v13510), and [1.34.13](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/blob/master/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-1.34.md#v13413) are available. November [patch release cherry-picks are due Friday, November 6](https://kubernetes.io/releases/patch-releases/#upcoming-monthly-releases), with releases targeted for November 10.

Kubernetes 1.34 [reaches end of life on October 27](https://kubernetes.io/releases/patch-releases/#134); move to a supported version.
