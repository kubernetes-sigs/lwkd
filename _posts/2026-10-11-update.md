## Developer News

Wei Fu ([@fuweid](https://github.com/fuweid)) has [joined SIG etcd as a lead](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9190).

SIG Apps has [added `workload-class` as a new subproject](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9197).

SIG Autoscaling has [added `model-serving-signals` as a new subproject](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9196).

WG LTS has been [wound down and its documentation archived](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9211) under `archive/wg-lts`.

SIG Architecture has [fixed inverted SPDY and WebSocket client guidance](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9150) in the developer guide; contributors building clients should re-read the corrected section.

The [Kubernetes SIG Meet & Greet at KubeCon NA 2026](https://groups.google.com/a/kubernetes.io/g/dev/c/ELCI9hCyAH4) will take place in Salt Lake City November 10 at 12:30 PM, giving contributors a chance to meet SIG leads in person before KubeCon sessions.

## Subprojects and Dependency Updates

* [cluster-api v1.14.3](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/cluster-api/releases/tag/v1.14.3): Fixes a race in KCP proxy DialContext, matches etcd members to machines being deleted by KCP, and bumps grpc to 1.83.2 for security advisories; also [v1.13.7](https://github.com/kubernetes-sigs/cluster-api/releases/tag/v1.13.7)
* [cri-o v1.37.2](https://github.com/cri-o/cri-o/releases/tag/v1.37.2): Switches release bundle SBOMs to SPDX 3.0.1 JSON-LD format
* [prometheus v3.13.4](https://github.com/prometheus/prometheus/releases/tag/v3.13.4): Fixes GO-2026-6348 HTTP/2 DATA frame fragmentation memory exhaustion and corruption of float native histograms in federation
* [nerdctl v2.4.1](https://github.com/containerd/nerdctl/releases/tag/v2.4.1): Fixes CDI support in compose, fixes port forwarding on Windows, and updates bundled containerd to 2.4.1
