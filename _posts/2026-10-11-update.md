## KEP of the Week

### [KEP-5945: DRA Optional Node Operations](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/blob/master/keps/sig-node/5945-dra-optional-node-operations/README.md)

Dynamic Resource Allocation (DRA) normally makes kubelet call a node-local driver
to prepare a device before a container starts and clean it up when its Pod ends.
Some controller-managed resources need neither call, yet still require a no-op
driver on every node. Others need preparation but clean up automatically.

KEP-5945 adds `skipNodeOperations` to `ResourceSlice` and carries it into
`ResourceClaim` allocation results. Kubelet skips a gRPC call only when all
devices from a driver in the claim mark that operation to skip. A driver can
skip `NodeUnprepareResources` alone or both it and `NodePrepareResources`.
Custom allocators must copy the field for the kubelet to apply this choice.

Troy Chiu drafted the KEP with SIG Node in May 2026 after discussion in the WG
Device Management meeting. It reached Alpha in v1.37 with the
`DRAOptionalNodeOperations` gate off by default. KEP 5945 is implementable; its
[Beta update merged September 28](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/pull/6372) targets
Beta in v1.38 with the gate on by default and Stable in v1.40. The
[enhancement issue](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/issues/5945) tracks
the work.
