## Developer News

SIG ContribEx has [updated the repo creation guidelines](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9232) and the [WG governance document](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9228) to clarify how Working Groups relate to code ownership and repository creation, giving future WG proposals a clearer path from charter to code.

SIG Architecture has [added notes on future conformance](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/pull/9227) in the developer guide, outlining how upcoming conformance work (including the newly formed WG Workload Conformance) will relate to existing conformance definitions.

SIG ContribEx is [coordinating KubeCon EU 2027 Project Pavilion and PR participation](https://groups.google.com/a/kubernetes.io/g/dev/c/WH4G2t9VAUw) for SIGs, WGs, Committees, and Subprojects; interested groups should submit the registration form by November 25.

The [Maintainer Summit NA 2026 unconference](https://groups.google.com/a/kubernetes.io/g/dev/c/hzQyj4rw3Yg) is accepting topic ideas; sessions are open community-led discussions and need only a topic, a note-taker, and a facilitator attending in Salt Lake City.

The SIG Scheduling leadership change proposed last week has [taken effect after lazy consensus closed on October 26](https://groups.google.com/a/kubernetes.io/g/dev/c/X-OG8zIphH8): Heba Elayoty is now a SIG Chair, Matt Matejczyk is a SIG Tech Lead, and Kensei Nakada has stepped down from Chair while remaining a Tech Lead.
