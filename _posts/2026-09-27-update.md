## Subprojects and Dependency Updates

* [containerd v2.4.1](https://github.com/containerd/containerd/releases/tag/v2.4.1): Fixes CVE-2026-53493, leaked tasks after failed container start, and container creation when SELinux relabeling is unsupported; also [v2.3.6](https://github.com/containerd/containerd/releases/tag/v2.3.6), [v2.2.9](https://github.com/containerd/containerd/releases/tag/v2.2.9), [v2.0.13](https://github.com/containerd/containerd/releases/tag/v2.0.13), [v1.7.36](https://github.com/containerd/containerd/releases/tag/v1.7.36)
* [prometheus v3.15.0](https://github.com/prometheus/prometheus/releases/tag/v3.15.0): Deprecates --log.level in favor of runtime.log_level, adds Unix Domain Socket scraping, stabilizes XOR2 float chunk encoding, and adds zstd scrape support
* [etcd v3.7.2](https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/releases/tag/v3.7.2): new patch release with cherry-picks of bugfixes, see [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/blob/main/CHANGELOG/CHANGELOG-3.7.md); also [v3.6.15](https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/releases/tag/v3.6.15) and [v3.5.34](https://github.com/etcd-io/etcd/releases/tag/v3.5.34)
