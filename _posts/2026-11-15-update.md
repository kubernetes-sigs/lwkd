## KEP of the Week

### [KEP-6386: Ephemeral Storage Support for Pod-Level Resource Specifications](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/blob/master/keps/sig-node/6386-pod-level-ephemeral-storage/README.md)

Pods can share disk-backed `emptyDir` volumes, but today ephemeral-storage
requests and limits are set per container. That makes it hard to budget shared
storage and can let an injected sidecar's limit unintentionally cap the whole
Pod. KEP-6386 proposes Pod-level ephemeral-storage requests and limits behind
the `PodLevelResourcesEphemeralStorage` feature gate. The design updates the
scheduler, quota and LimitRanger handling, and kubelet eviction manager;
the existing accounting of writable layers, container logs, and disk-backed
`emptyDir` volumes stays the same. In-place resizing is out of scope.

[ndixita](https://github.com/ndixita) authored the KEP for SIG Node, with SIG
Storage participating. The proposal builds on [KEP-2837](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/blob/master/keps/sig-node/2837-pod-level-resource-spec/README.md),
which introduced Pod-level CPU and memory resources and deferred ephemeral
storage. The [Kubernetes implementation PR](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/pull/143777)
is open. KEP-6386 is `implementable`, targets Alpha in v1.38, Beta in v1.39,
and Stable in v1.41; its [enhancement issue](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/issues/6386)
tracks the work.
