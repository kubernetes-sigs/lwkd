## KEP of the Week

### [KEP-5502: EmptyDir Volume Permission Mode](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/blob/master/keps/sig-storage/5502-emptydir-volume-mode/README.md)

KEP-5502 adds an optional `mode` field to `EmptyDirVolumeSource`, allowing permissions such as `0750` or the sticky bit (`01777`) without an init container running `chmod`. With `EmptyDirVolumeMode` enabled, kubelet applies the requested mode when creating the directory; omitting it preserves `0777`. Values must be between `0000` and `01777`. The field has no effect on Windows, and `fsGroup` can modify the resulting permissions.

[Nispriha Jagan](https://github.com/nispriha) and [Neeraj Krishna Gopalakrishna](https://github.com/ngopalak-redhat) authored the KEP for SIG Storage with SIG Node, building on a [2022 request for sticky-bit support](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/issues/110835). The [KEP merged in June](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/pull/6200), and its [Alpha implementation is merged](https://github.com/kubernetes/kubernetes/pull/140244) for v1.37. The [Beta KEP update merged October 5](https://github.com/kubernetes/enhancements/pull/6437) and targets Beta in v1.38; the KEP's status remains `implementable`.
