## Election Updates
Voting is now open for the [2026 Kubernetes Steering Committee election](https://elections.k8s.io/). Six candidates are running this year, including [Sean McGinnis](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-stmcginnis.md) (stmcginnis), [Michael McCune](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-elmiko.md) (elmiko), [Priyanka Saggu](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-priyankasaggu11929.md) (priyankasaggu11929), [Janet Kuo](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-janetkuo.md) (janetkuo), [Kaslin Fields](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-kaslin.md) (kaslin), and [Jack Francis](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/main/elections/steering/2026/candidate-jackfrancis.md) (jackfrancis). Voting will remain open until October 1, when the election closes at the end of the day AoE.

All Kubernetes org members are eligible to vote this year due to GitHub contributions not being accurately tracked during the first four months of 2026. Members of the SRC or CoCC are also eligible to vote. Eligibility can be checked at [elections.k8s.io](https://elections.k8s.io/) or in the full voter list in the [election repository](https://github.com/kubernetes/community/blob/master/elections/steering/2026/voters.yaml).
